Manufacturers/importers of base oil and lubrication oil, importers of used oil, brands selling oil under private or co-branded labels, and producers selling domestically manufactured or imported oil.

## Resource

EPR Battery E-book — simplified guide to CPCB rules, timelines, and best practices.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What categories of batteries are covered under Battery EPR?**
CPCB has defined four battery categories: Portable Batteries, Electric Vehicle (EV) Batteries, Automotive Batteries, Industrial Batteries — each with separate collection targets and reporting requirements.

**What documents are required for CPCB EPR registration?**
Business registration (CIN, GST, PAN); authorised signatory KYC; sales and import data; details of battery chemistry and categories; proof of collection and recycling tie-ups.

**What are the Battery EPR collection targets?**
70% of batteries sold in FY 2022–23 must be collected by 2025. From FY 2024–25 onwards, producers must collect 90% of the batteries sold two years prior.

**What happens if EPR targets are not met?**
Environmental Compensation (EC) charges, public disclosure of violations, obligation carry forward with interest. Paying EC does not exempt future target fulfilment.

**How does the credit or fulfilment process work?**
Credits are generated only by CPCB-registered recyclers and are visible on the CPCB portal. Producers can claim fulfilment only when these credits are transferred to their CPCB account; recyclers must upload documentation to validate recycling.

**Are different battery chemistries treated differently?**
Yes. CPCB classifies batteries by chemistry and use-case, affecting labeling, safety handling, target obligations, and disposal methods. Common types: Lithium-ion, Lead-acid, Nickel-Cadmium, Zinc-carbon.

**Is reverse logistics covered under EPR Battery compliance?**
Yes — producers must establish a reverse collection mechanism via collection centres, take-back systems, or authorised third parties, declared and approved as part of the EPR plan submitted to CPCB.

**Who needs to comply with Battery EPR rules?**
Battery manufacturers, importers (including vehicle importers), OEMs that use batteries in their products, online sellers of battery-powered equipment, and refurbishers/recyclers (for registration/reporting).

**Can Recykal help with quarterly filings and audits?**
Yes — Recykal only partners with CPCB-authorised recyclers; all credits are validated, audit-traceable, and registered on the official portal.

**How long does onboarding take?**
3–5 working days, depending on document readiness and internal approvals.
