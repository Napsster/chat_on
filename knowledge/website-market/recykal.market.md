## Buyer Obligations

## Dispatch & Documentation

Sellers must upload, per shipment, before marking it "Dispatched": e-way bill, empty and full weighbridge slips, ≥3 loading photographs, and tax invoice. Multiple trucks against one Purchase Order may be split into multiple shipments. Status auto-updates to "In-Transit" with a dispatch timestamp. After delivery, Sellers can view the Buyer's uploaded Proof of Delivery, QC Report, and any Debit Note.

## Quality Control & Deductions

If no parameter is breached, the shipment is marked "Delivered" and payment proceeds. If breached, the Buyer generates a debit note; the Seller may accept (payment proceeds net of deduction) or reject (shipment marked "Rejected," triggering dispute resolution). Full rejection means the vehicle returns to the Seller at the Seller's cost.

## Payment & Escrow

Buyer funds escrow before dispatch. After QC acceptance, Buyer uploads bank payment advice (Recykal extracts UTR/invoice/amount via AI OCR for validation). Payment is due within the agreed credit period. Debit notes must be uploaded within 48 hours of Seller's acceptance of a deduction; a variance alert is raised if the uploaded amount exceeds the calculated deduction. Late payment can trigger account suspension.

Onboarding may include offline verification of credentials, financial standing, and operational capacity, directly or via third-party agencies. Third-party logistics partners act as independent contractors; Recykal is not liable for their delays/damage/loss, force majeure, or conditions beyond its control. Buyers must notify of visible damage/shortage within 48 hours of delivery, or the goods are deemed accepted in good condition.
